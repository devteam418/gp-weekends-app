# GP Weekends Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

GP Weekends is an unofficial motorsport companion application providing race schedules, results, standings, track information, statistics and other motorsport-related content.

---

## 🔒 Personal Data

GP Weekends does not require user registration and does not collect, store, sell or share personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers or locations.

## 📡 Third-Party Data Sources

GP Weekends retrieves publicly available motorsport data from third-party services, including but not limited to OpenF1, Jolpica and Undercut F1 APIs.

These services are used solely to provide race information, results, standings, schedules and statistics within the application.

## 📈 Analytics and Advertising

GP Weekends does not display advertising and does not sell user data.

GP Weekends does not use tracking technologies for advertising purposes.

## 💾 Data Storage

No personal user information is stored by GP Weekends.

## 🔄 Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
